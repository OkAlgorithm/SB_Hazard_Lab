# Lecture concept artwork

The overview is the user-approved **Kazakh Workshop with Soviet-Era Lathes** concept artwork supplied for this course. The loaded-trolley and near-miss frames were generated from that reference for chapters L3–L5. They are illustrative training scenes, not photographs of actual workplace events.

The three browser images are compressed WebP copies of those concept frames. Hotspots and teaching notes are chapter-specific and defined in `src/lecture-art.ts`.
